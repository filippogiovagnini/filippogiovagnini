# Hi, I'm Filippo! 👨🏼‍💻

I'm a Maths PhD candidate @ Imperial College London, working at the intersection of stochastic analysis, stochastic PDEs, interacting particle systems, and mathematical questions around modern machine learning.

My research is mainly about how complex random systems behave in limiting regimes: particle approximations of stochastic fluid equations, zero-noise limits of stochastic regularizations, and universality phenomena in wide neural networks.

I also build applied ML systems, with experience in NLP pipelines, transformer-based modelling, and model evaluation.

## 🧠 Selected work

- **[Uniform particle approximation for 3D Navier--Stokes--alpha models with advection noise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12960)**  
  Particle methods for stochastic vorticity dynamics and convergence to SPDE limits.

- **[Non-selection of Lagrangian trajectories in the zero-noise limit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07096)**  
  Construction of stochastic regularizations whose vanishing-noise limits do not select a unique deterministic flow.

- **[Universality in deep neural networks via Lindeberg exchange](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02771)**  
  Quantitative comparison of non-Gaussian and Gaussian wide neural networks using layerwise replacement.

- **[NeuroGolf 2026 Kaggle Silver Medal](https://github.com/MaxGhi8/neurogolf2026)**  
  Built an agentic workflow for compact ONNX solvers under exact-output, parameter, and memory constraints.
